
# 🚲 Bike Sales Data Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

This project is a dynamic Excel dashboard built to analyze customer data and bike purchasing patterns. It helps stakeholders understand key drivers behind bike purchases, such as demographics, income, occupation, commute habits, and more.

## 📊 Project Overview
The dashboard provides interactive visual insights into:
*  Average income levels based on bike purchases and gender
*  Distribution of bike purchases across different age groups
*  Regional and marital status segmentation
*  Education level & occupation breakdown
*  Commute distance and its correlation with purchase decisions

  ## 🔐 Key Requirements
*  Identify key customer segments more likely to purchase bikes (e.g., by age, gender, region, or income).
*  Understand how commute patterns influence purchase behavior to guide marketing and product placement.
*  Measure the impact of education, occupation, and marital status on sales performance.
*  Optimize marketing and sales efforts by targeting regions and demographics with the highest conversion potential.
*  Track customer purchasing trends over time to support inventory and demand planning.


## 🧰 Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel 
* Pivot Tables
* Slicers for interactivity
* Charts (Bar, Line, Combo)
* Conditional formatting
## 💡 Key Insights
* Middle-aged customers are the most frequent buyers.
* Higher income males tend to purchase bikes more.
* Customers with management and professional occupations are strong segments.
* Commute distance plays a notable role—those commuting 0–1 or 5–10 miles show higher purchase rates.
* Regional and marital status filters help reveal demographic-specific trends.
## 📚 Learning Outcomes
This project helped reinforce:
* Effective dashboard design in Excel
* Interactive reporting using slicers
* Data storytelling with conditional formatting and chart combinations
* Understanding business use cases from raw data
