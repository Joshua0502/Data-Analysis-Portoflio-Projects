# 📊 Sales Data Analysis Dashboard
An interactive and visually engaging Sales Dashboard built using Tableau to explore key business metrics such as sales, profit, and quantity sold across different product categories, customers, and locations. 
 [Here](https://public.tableau.com/views/SalesDashboard_17517338283360/SalesDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

## 🧩 Project Overview
The dashboard helps answer critical business questions like:
* Which products or sub-categories drive the most sales and profits?
* How do sales and profit trends evolve over time?
* Are there underperforming items contributing to losses?
* What was the highest and lowest performing month compared to last year?

## 🧑‍💼 Key Requirements (Customer’s Perspective)
*  Display a summary of total sales, profits and quantity for the current year and the previous year.
*  Present the data for each KPI on a monthly basis for both the current year and the previous year.
*  Identify months with highest and lowest sales and make them easy to recognize.
*  Compare sales performance by different product subcategories for the current year and the previous year.
*  Include a comparison of sales with profit.
*  Present weekly sales and profit data for the current year.
*  Display the average weekly values.
*  Highlight weeks that are above and below the average to draw attention to sales & profit performance.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features
* Highlighted highest and lowest performing months using color-coded indicators
* Comparative bars for 2023 vs 2022 sales
* Split view of sales vs. profits by sub-category
* Trend lines showing weekly performance against averages
* KPI Tiles for total metrics with YoY deltas

## 📌 Key Insights
* Total Sales (2023): $733K — a 20.4% increase over the previous year
* Total Profit: $93K — up 14.24% YoY.
* Phones, Chairs, and Binders are top revenue-generating sub-categories.
* Highest-performing month and lowest month are clearly marked for all KPIs.

## 💻 Tools & Technologies
* Tableau – for data visualization & dashboard design
* Excel – for initial data exploration and cleaning
* Calculated Fields – to highlight YoY comparisons, performance thresholds, and trend lines

## 📚 Learning Outcomes
* Built end-to-end dashboard from raw Excel data
* Applied calculated fields and trend comparisons
* Practiced visual storytelling and design principles using Tableau
* Developed insights for business decision-making with visual impact
